# Time Off Policy — Nimbus Freight Co.

*Internal policy document. Effective January 2026.*

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate of **1.5 days per
month**, for a maximum of **18 days per calendar year**. Part-time employees
who work at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis at half
the full-time rate.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over up to **5 unused PTO days** into the following
calendar year. Any unused days beyond that cap are forfeited on
**December 31st**. There is no cap on carryover for employees who have been
with the company for more than 7 years — they may carry over their full
unused balance.

## Requesting Time Off

PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least **10 business
days** in advance for any request longer than 3 consecutive days. Requests
of 3 days or fewer require only **2 business days** of advance notice.
Manager approval is required for all requests; the manager has
**3 business days** to approve or deny a request before it auto-escalates
to the department director.

## Blackout Periods

PTO requests are not accepted during the last week of each fiscal quarter
(the final 7 calendar days of March, June, September, and December), except
for emergency leave, which is handled separately under the Emergency Leave
Policy.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and is not subject to the accrual
cap above. Employees receive **10 sick days per year**, which do not carry
over and are not paid out upon termination.
